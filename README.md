# 🛒 Sales and Profit Analysis – Amazon Orders

This data analysis project explores Amazon order data to uncover key sales and profitability patterns across products and regions. The goal is to help improve strategic decision-making for inventory, marketing, and product focus.

---

## 🎯 Project Goals

- Identify top-performing product categories and products
- Understand customer buying behavior through quantity patterns
- Uncover which cities and states bring in the most sales/profit
- Analyze monthly sales trends over time
- Calculate and compare average profit margins by category

---

## 📊 Key Insights

- **Chairs** generated the highest **revenue**, but **Copiers** gave the highest **profit**.
- **Staples** were the most **frequently purchased** product by quantity.
- **Los Angeles**, **Seattle**, and **San Francisco** are the **most profitable cities**.
- **California** leads in both **number of orders** and **sales volume**.
- **Profit margins** vary greatly between categories, helping identify where to scale or cut back.

---

## 📈 Visualizations Summary

All insights are backed by meaningful visualizations:
- Horizontal bar charts for category-wise sales and profit
- Product quantity visualizations
- City-wise and state-wise performance graphs
- Time-based sales trend using `order_month` column

---

## ✅ Conclusion

This analysis provides a detailed snapshot of what drives sales and profit on Amazon:
- Focus on **high-profit, high-demand products** (e.g., Copiers, Chairs)
- Invest more in **top-performing cities and states**
- Refine strategies for **low-margin high-volume** products
- Use **monthly trends** to plan seasonal promotions or stock-up periods.
